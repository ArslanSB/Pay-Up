Tip jar: three directions

Each page shows the dashboard and the owner's jar screen at phone width. The tap button works in all three so you can feel the interaction. These are throwaway mockups, not the app.

A. Shelf

Illustrated glass jars that fill with coins. Calm, tactile, kitchen-counter feel.

B. Receipt

Each jar is a printed receipt with a total due. Wry, honest, itemised. One red paid stamp.
Loud

C. Loud

Poster energy. Hot pink pay button, hard shadows, chunky type. Pay up, no guilt.

Not shown: the public read-only page (the jar screen without the buttons), the sign-in landing, and the create/edit form. Those follow whichever direction wins.
